以汉乐府《江南》「鱼戏莲叶间」为灵感的外朗石砚系列。砚面上鱼与莲的线条在石纹中若隐若现，是水墨意境与石刻技艺的完美交融。浅浮雕鱼纹在墨汁浸润时仿佛游动起来。

A Wailang stone inkstone series inspired by the Han-dynasty poem 'Fish Among Lotus Leaves'. Fish and lotus lines emerge and recede within the stone's veining — a perfect fusion of ink-wash aesthetics and stone-carving craft.

The Story

Creative Story

诗里写「鱼戏莲叶东，鱼戏莲叶西，鱼戏莲叶南，鱼戏莲叶北」——老赵觉得砚台也应该是这样：不同角度有不同的美。他刻意在砚台的四个方向做了不同深浅的浮雕，让使用者在研墨的时候，随着手腕转动能看到鱼在不同方位的莲叶间穿梭。
The poem goes: 'Fish play east of the lotus, west of the lotus, south of the lotus, north of the lotus.' Zhao thought inkstones should be the same — beautiful from every angle. He carved reliefs of different depths on all four sides, so as you grind ink, you see fish darting between lotus leaves in every direction.
