About the Atelier

A Sculptor-Led Manufacturer, Not a Trading Company

Founded in 2016 by Zhao Yanqing, a National-Level Industrial Art Designer and Sichuan Provincial Master Craftsman, Yanqing Sculpture combines original design ability with in-house carving and fabrication. We operate two studios: a design studio in Chongqing and a stone-carving workshop in Baoxing — home of Baoxing white marble and the Muping inkstone (Wailang stone) intangible heritage. Over the past decade we have delivered 30+ public sculpture projects across China for governments, universities, parks and real-estate developers.

How It Works

From Brief to Installation

1 · Consult & Brief

Share your idea, site, size, material and budget. We reply with feasibility and a rough quote.

2 · Design & 3D

We develop sketches and a 3D model so you can review the form before carving begins.

3 · Carving & Fabrication

In-house carving, casting or fabrication with progress photos at each stage.

4 · Finish & Quality

Surface finishing, anti-stain treatment and a pre-shipment inspection report.

5 · Pack & Ship

Wood-crate packing, sea or air freight, full insurance and destination clearance support.
